What are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners to make their websites work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

We also use other technologies, such as pixels, scripts, and tags (collectively referred to as "Trackers"), which perform similar functions as cookies.

Why We Use Cookies

We use first-party and third-party cookies for several reasons:

  • Necessary Cookies: To enable the basic functionality of our Application.
  • Performance and Analytics Cookies: To analyze how our Application is accessed, used, or performing to provide you with a better user experience and to maintain, operate, and continually improve the Application.
  • Functionality Cookies: To remember choices you make and provide enhanced, more personalized features.
